A free paycheck budget you can set up in 10 minutes

The Paycheck Budget Starter is a small spreadsheet for planning one paycheck at a time: take-home pay, fixed bills, savings, and what's left over. The formulas are done for you. It's free, and you don't need to give an email address.

Works in Google Sheets (free Google account) and Excel. The download opens in Google Sheets if you upload it to Google Drive.

What's inside

  • Paycheck: take-home pay plus any other income
  • Fixed bills: due dates, amounts, and a Paid? dropdown that greys out paid bills
  • Savings & debt: pay yourself first, before spending
  • Everyday spending: groceries, gas and fun money
  • Summary: leftover and a status line that turns red if you're over

How to use it

  1. Make a copy (or download the file) so it's yours to edit.
  2. Type your take-home pay for one paycheck.
  3. List the bills you'll pay from that paycheck, then your savings and debt payments.
  4. Plan groceries, gas and fun money, then aim for a leftover of $0, so every dollar has a job.
  5. Mark bills as Paid as you go. Duplicate the tab for your next paycheck.

The sample numbers are only examples. This is a planning tool, not financial advice.
